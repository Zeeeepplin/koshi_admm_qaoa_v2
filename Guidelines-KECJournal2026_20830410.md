# **gUIDELINES FOR SUBMISSION OF MANUSCRIPT**

## **General Information**

Author's manuscript is the ultimate basis for the selection in the
conference. The author(s) must be abided by the mentioned guidelines and
formats which go through a series of reviews. Acceptance/rejection of
the manuscript will be notified to the author(s). The author(s) are
expected to comply with the suggestions made by the reviewers. The
decision of the editorial board will be final.

Accepted manuscripts will be published in the KEC Journal 2026. Accepted
manuscripts shall not be published elsewhere in the same form, in
English or in any other language, without the written consent of KEC
Journal Organizing Committee.

## **Manuscript Preparation**

- The manuscript should be limited to **8** pages.

- The templates in MS Word are available on the KEC website.

- Use bold face, italics, subscripts, superscripts, etc wherever needed.

- Color drawings, diagrams and graphs accompanying the manuscript should
  be neat and the labelling including the units and its values should be
  clear. The lines and details of drawings, diagrams and graphs should
  be clear and distinct.

- Color photographs should be sharp and clear, preferably with high
  resolution and quality.

- All mathematical equations/ formulae should be typed with proper
  symbols leaving no room for confusion between capital and lowercase
  letters, subscripts and superscripts, etc. Superscripts, subscripts
  and dashes should be mentioned in the proper format. Thus, please
  mention:

> *e^k-1\ ^*and not *ek-1*
>
> *M ^2^, N~xy~', C~2~* and not *M2, Nxy', C2*
>
> *M~B~', σ~sy~', f~c~'* and not *M'~B~, σ'sy, f'c*

- If you need to use abbreviations, example u/s and d/s for upstream and
  downstream, the author shall mention the full form in its first
  appearance.

## **Reference Preparation** 

Refer conference manuscript template for the reference section. Only the
articles published in journals, books, newspapers, authenticated
reports, etc. would be entertained as reference for manuscript.

## **SoftCopy**

Author should submit a softcopy of their manuscript in *pdf* format*.*
